04 — About

Behind the lens.

Trashpanda Garage started the way most good things do — in a garage, with borrowed light and too much curiosity.

Today it's a studio practice built on one belief: a photograph should feel like a frame from a film you want to keep watching. Every session is planned like a story — location, light and wardrobe working toward a single mood.

The process is slow on purpose. Fewer sessions, more attention. Every image that leaves the studio is finished by hand.
